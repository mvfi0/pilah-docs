● The backend half of PIL-451 is done: pilah-be #137
  (https://github.com/bank-sampah-PILAH/pilah-be/pull/137), opened against
  staging and assigned to you. CI has just started on it.

  What it does: a new PATCH /api/v1/onboarding/role lets an account change its
  role, but only while onboarding hasn't saved anything for that role yet.
  - Allowed: it returns a fresh session, the same payload as a Google login. The
    new token names the new role, and the app can route on next_step as it does
    after registering.
  - Refused with 409 role_locked: once the profile is complete, a bank sampah
    has been registered (even one still pending), or a nasabah membership is
    linked.
  - Every real change is recorded in a new user_role_change_log table
    (migration 0029): whose account, from which role, to which, and when.

  Two things the work turned up:
  - Before this fix, a pengelola with a pending bank sampah could have been
    switched to nasabah, leaving the bank orphaned. The check and the write are
    now one database statement, so a bank registration landing at the same
    moment can't slip through.
  - A nasabah linked to a pengurus-entered membership used to hit an unhandled
